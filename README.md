# Dinosaurs Landscaping Plan

I'd like to plan out a modern, professional website for a lawn care and landscaping company called "Dinosaurs Landscaping" before we build it. Let's use this as the plan.

COMPANY OVERVIEW

Name: Dinosaurs Landscaping

Based in Savannah, Georgia

Also services surrounding areas: Isle of Hope, Dutch Island, Pooler, Garden City, Georgetown, and Wilmington Island

I already have a logo, phone number, and email — use placeholders for these ([LOGO], [PHONE NUMBER], [EMAIL]) so I can drop in my real assets and info later

Tone: trustworthy, hardworking, locally-rooted, professional but approachable — this is a small local business people should feel comfortable inviting onto their property

DESIGN DIRECTION

Clean, modern, airy layout with lots of white space

Primary palette: a clean white background paired with a soft, light pastel green as the core accent color (should feel fresh and "clean lawn," not neon or saturated)

Feel free to bring in a couple of complementary colors beyond white and pastel green — e.g. a deeper forest green or warm neutral/earthy tone for contrast, text, and CTAs — but white and pastel green should anchor the palette throughout

Bold, readable typography that feels modern and trustworthy, not playful/cartoonish (subtle nod to the "Dinosaurs" name is fine, but keep it tasteful)

High-quality lawn/landscape imagery (use nice stock-style placeholder images of manicured lawns, hedges, pressure-washed driveways, etc.)

Fully responsive (mobile-first, since many customers will call from their phone)

Sticky header with logo, nav, and a prominent "Get a Free Quote" button and click-to-call phone number
